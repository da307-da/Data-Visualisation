This visualisation report contains an analysis of teams from the Czech First League over the course of the 2024/25 season. 

The report assesses and compares teams in the league in terms of their attacking and defensive abilities.

Before completing this report, a raw Excel spreadsheet produced by StatsBomb was given to me. The spreadsheet records every single match event minute by minute across multiple matches.

My assignment was to translate the data from the spreadsheet into well-defined metrics used to compare performance levels of all the teams in the league. This process involved me researching and learning about the commonly used statistic 'obv', which is explained in the report.

The metrics I used were attacking threat (presented with a bar graph), attack origins (displayed using a heatmap), defensive disruption (presented with a bar graph), and overall defense-attack balance using a similarity system that maps attacking threat up against defensive disruption.

I was able to link the reason for the most attack-threatening team's success to their attack origins being evenly distributed across the pitch, making them more versatile.

I was able to use my own football understanding to compose the metrics. For example the attacking threat metric was made up of a range of different components, such as net obv in the attacking third, final third pass success rate, positive obv rate, average xG per shot and average obv per pass. These components were weighted based on the importance I felt they carried to attacking threat.
